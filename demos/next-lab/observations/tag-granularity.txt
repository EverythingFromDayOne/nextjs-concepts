Inner stamp is module state bumped only by the invalidate-inner probe.

finding: invalidating **inner** refreshed **outer** as well (new outerGeneratedAt, new
inner stamp, ~180ms miss). This is **not** the composition trap the draft assumed —
the outer entry did not keep serving a result built from stale inner data.
Likely mechanism: the outer entry collects the inner tag when it calls the inner
cached scope, so expiring `inner` expires the outer entry too.

Control: revalidateTag('outer', {expire:0}) after that → outer recomputes quickly
(~1ms) while reusing the warm inner HIT (same inner generatedAt) — outer and inner
are distinct entries; only the tag sets overlap via composition.
