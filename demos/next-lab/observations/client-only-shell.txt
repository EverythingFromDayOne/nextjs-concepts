## 2. .next/server/app/sketchpad.html — trimmed to <main>…</main>

<main><h1>Sketchpad</h1><p>Draw with a pointer. Strokes are counted locally.</p><div><canvas style="width:100%;height:300px;border:1px solid #ccc"></canvas><p>0<!-- --> strokes</p></div></main>

- The `<h1>` and the prose ARE in the HTML, as expected.
- CORRECTION to the article's working assumption: the `<canvas>` element
  ITSELF IS in the HTML. `Sketchpad` is a plain `'use client'` component
  (no `ssr: false`), so it is hydration-deferred, not never-server-rendered
  — it renders on the server like any other component. What's missing is
  not the tag but everything the component's effects and handlers produce:
  no `width`/`height` attributes (set in a `useEffect` from
  `devicePixelRatio`), and "0 strokes" rather than any drawn state, because
  no pointer events have fired yet. This is the distinction the article
  draws between hydration-deferred (content exists, starts empty) and
  `ssr: false` (content does not exist, replaced by the `loading` fallback)
  — sketchpad is the first kind, client-only/heavy-widget is the second.
